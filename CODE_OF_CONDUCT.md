# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We commit to a respectful, evidence-oriented environment, where claims are argued and verified.

## Expected Behavior

- Aim critique at ideas
- Provide evidence for claims
- Be explicit about uncertainty
- Respect cultural, philosophical, and scientific diversity
- Keep collaboration constructive

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks
- Deliberate misinformation
- Repeated bad-faith engagement
- Publishing private data without consent

## Enforcement

Project maintainers edit, hide or remove contributions that violate this policy.
Repeated or severe violations bring temporary or permanent exclusion from collaboration channels, at the maintainers' decision.

## Reporting

For conduct concerns, open a private maintainer contact request via repository issue with title:
`[private] conduct report`
